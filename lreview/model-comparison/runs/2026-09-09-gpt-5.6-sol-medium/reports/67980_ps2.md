## Findings

### 1. `lustre/llite/statahead.c` (line 2347)

(defect) Can a non-consecutive suffix reset `sfi_match_count` here? As written, this branch only updates `sfi_index`, so separate short runs accumulate toward the threshold. For example, with `match_hit=4`, suffixes `1,2,100,101,200,201,202` produce a match even though the longest consecutive run has only three names. That starts a shared statahead thread for a workload that does not meet the consecutive-name requirement.
